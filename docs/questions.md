# Analysis questions

These are the questions guiding the M5 Walmart sales analysis.

1. How have total unit sales and estimated sales value changed over time? Are there clear seasonal patterns, year-over-year growth, or long-term trends?

2. Which states and stores generate the highest and lowest unit sales and estimated sales value? How large is the performance gap between top and bottom performers?

3. Which product categories and departments drive the most and least unit sales and estimated sales value?

4. Which stores, categories, or departments are improving or declining over time? How do year-over-year growth rates and recent performance compare with earlier periods?

5. How do selling prices relate to unit demand? When prices change, how does demand respond, and which categories or products appear most price-sensitive?

6. What impact do holidays, special events, and SNAP periods have on sales volume? Which events create the largest demand lifts overall, by category, and by state?

7. Can a simple 28-day sales forecast at a useful aggregation level outperform a baseline forecast? What estimated sales value does the forecast represent, and how could improved forecast accuracy support business planning?
